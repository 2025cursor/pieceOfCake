About this fan guide

Maintained by: Piece Of Cake Online Fan Guide. Corrections and rights questions are handled at bigdata20182023@gmail.com.

Piece Of Cake Online Fan Guide is an independent reference site for players who want a short explanation of the merge board, customer orders, and cafe restoration in Piece of Cake: Merge and Bake.

How we publish

We separate public game facts from our own strategy observations. Game details are checked against the linked CrazyGames listing and are updated when the public build changes. We do not present this site as the developer, publisher, or official support channel.

Corrections and updates

If you find an inaccurate statement, an outdated control, or a rights concern, please use the contact page. We review factual corrections before changing the guide.
