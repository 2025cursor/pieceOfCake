How facts are checked

Game facts are compared with the public CrazyGames listing. The current public listing identifies HG POINT LTD as the developer, describes Emily helping restore a family cafe, and publishes the drag-to-merge control. It also describes toast, egg, salami, sandwiches, customer orders, restoration, daily missions, special orders, boosters, and new areas. This site does not turn those descriptions into a fixed recipe tree or claim hidden costs, timers, offline progress, or cross-device synchronization.

Correction policy

If an item name, control, source link, or screenshot description becomes inaccurate, email the page URL and the correction to bigdata20182023@gmail.com. The guide will record material corrections here after review. For game account or payment support, use the platform or developer listed in the source page.

Earlier site work

The first version of this site was a single long page with repeated game-name phrases and unsupported game-system claims. Those claims were removed in the October 2026 remediation. The current content deliberately separates public facts, planning observations, and unconfirmed features.
